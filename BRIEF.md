## Grammar
Custom grammar: observation notebook. A personal introduction leads to a desk connecting real evidence, then an inspectable project archive, a dated experience ledger, and a contact letter.
Nav: nonfixed paper masthead with chapter anchors. Hero: invitation beside a labelled photographic character inspiration. Ending: contact letter with actual address and social links.
Constraints: one observation desk, actual project images with factual labels, local inspectable details, chronological experience; prohibit continuous camera travel, repeated pinned text scenes, autoplay media, fake psychological diagnosis, invented achievements, and magnetic closing buttons.
The planned Gallery label was corrected during review because the page does not begin with object one and end with a museum label. A notebook describes the actual structure and carries its own constraints.
Other grammars: filmic one-shot disallows useful jumping; chaptered editorial requires a media-free title page; live surface treats the whole portfolio as software; continuous world needs geography that the evidence does not have; typographic monolith discards the real photos; gallery/catalog excludes the separate personal introduction; split stage needs two competing claims; rhythmic cutlist is too abrupt for reading roles and documentation.
